# Changelog

Each version's section becomes the notes of its release.

## 0.2.0

- **Talk to villagers** (when learning Korean or English, for now): right-clicking a villager asks
  whether to **Trade** or **Conversate**. In a conversation, press **Speak** and say something, or
  type it, and the villager answers in the language you are learning, out loud with your Windows
  voice. It understands greetings and names, how you are, its job, where things are, the weather,
  bread, the time, colors, safety and goodbyes, and answers from what is really there: its own job,
  the real weather and time of day, an iron golem nearby. Korean lines show the romanization and
  English translation, a hint suggests what to say back, and **Phrases** lists things to start
  with. It even sells you a bread for an emerald. Sneak and right-click to trade straight away.
- **English** can be learned too, so there are 14 languages.
- **Show pronunciation under names**: a checkbox in the Practice Language menu (also "Pronunciation"
  in the settings) hides the pinyin, romanization or reading under names.
- Fixed: what was heard ran its words together in German, Russian, Dutch and the other languages
  written with spaces, except Spanish and French.
- New settings: "Talk to villagers", "Villagers speak out loud" and "Pronunciation".

## 0.1.0

- First release: the first time you pick up an item, say its name out loud in Mandarin Chinese,
  Spanish, French, Korean, Russian, Dutch, Latin, Tagalog, Japanese, Bosnian, Portuguese,
  Vietnamese or German. Speech recognition runs on your computer (whisper.cpp).
- A wrong answer shows what was heard, and a Windows voice says the name.
- Items list and Practice Language buttons in the game menu, a settings screen and `/langmod`
  commands.
